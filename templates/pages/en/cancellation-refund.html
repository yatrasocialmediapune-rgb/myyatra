How to cancel

Send your cancellation request in writing on WhatsApp ({{site.phone}}) or by email to {{site.email}}. The date we receive your message is used to calculate charges.

Cancellation charges

  • 30 days or more before travel: 10% of the package cost.
  • 15 to 29 days before travel: 25% of the package cost.
  • 7 to 14 days before travel: 50% of the package cost.
  • Less than 7 days before travel or no-show: 100% of the package cost.

Flight, train, houseboat, festival-period and peak-season bookings may follow stricter supplier rules, which we share with your quote.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–14 working days.
  • Bank or payment gateway charges, if any, are deducted from the refund.
  • No refund is due for unused services once the trip has started, such as skipped sightseeing or meals.

Changes by us

If we have to cancel a trip for reasons within our control, you will receive a full refund of the amount paid or a credit for a future trip, as you prefer.

Date changes

We are happy to help move your trip to new dates, subject to availability. Any difference in price or supplier charges will apply.
